Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This page covers what the Omninator browser extension ("the extension", "we", "us") collects, why, and where it goes. Installing or using the extension means you accept these terms.

1. What gets stored

There's no account or sign-in built into Omninator, and no backend server behind it today. The extension keeps the following locally, in your browser's own extension storage:

  • Your settings — destination folder, model, aspect ratio, output count, quality, file-naming scheme, and watermark/refresh preferences.
  • Your prompts and batch/queue history — enough to resume, retry, or reopen a run you started earlier.

2. Where it goes

Nowhere but your own machine. All of it lives in your browser's local extension storage — Omninator doesn't send any of it to us or to a third-party server.

3. What we never see

  • Your prompts or the images/videos they produce. Prompts go straight to your own Google Flow session; results are generated inside your own Flow account and downloaded to your device. None of that passes through us.
  • No browsing history, location, health information, or personal messages are collected.
  • No payment information is collected right now, because purchasing isn't live yet — see Pricing on the homepage.

4. Payments, once they exist

Once Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime purchases open up, a third-party payment provider will handle them as Merchant of Record, with card details entered directly on that provider's own checkout page — never passing through the extension. This section will name the provider once that's live.

5. Who else is involved

  • Google — specifically the Google Flow service Omninator automates using your own session and quota.

6. How long data sticks around

Whatever's stored locally disappears when you clear the extension's storage or remove the extension itself.

7. Security

Nothing transmitted or stored is ever perfectly secure, but the simplest safeguard here is structural: there's very little to secure, since almost nothing leaves your device.

8. Age restriction

Omninator isn't built for, marketed to, or intended for use by children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age where you live).

9. Updates to this page

This policy may change over time, especially once payments launch. Any meaningful change will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on GitHub.

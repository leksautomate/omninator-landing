Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Omninator browser extension ("the extension", "we", "us") collects, why, and how it is handled. By installing or using the extension you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

Omninator does not require an account or sign-in of its own, and has no backend server today. It stores the following locally, on your own device, using the browser's built-in extension storage:

  • Your settings — folder, model, aspect ratio, count, quality, file naming, and watermark/refresh options.
  • Your prompts and batch/queue history — so you can resume, retry, or re-open a previous run.

2. Where your data goes

Everything above stays on your device, in your browser's local extension storage. Omninator does not transmit it to us or to any third-party server.

3. What we do NOT collect or access

  • Your prompts and generated images. Prompts are sent only to your own Google Flow session to generate images and videos. Generated results are created in your Google Flow account and downloaded to your device. We never receive, store, or access them.
  • We do not collect your browsing history, location, health data, or personal communications.
  • We do not currently collect any payment information — purchases are not yet live (see Pricing on the homepage).

4. Payments (once available)

When Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime purchases go live, they will be processed by a third-party payment provider acting as Merchant of Record. Card details will be handled entirely on that provider's hosted checkout — never seen or stored by the extension itself. This policy will be updated with the provider's name once checkout is live.

5. Third parties

We rely on this service provider to operate the extension:

  • Google — the Google Flow service the extension automates on your behalf, using your own session and quota.

6. Data retention

Data stored locally on your device is removed when you clear the extension's storage or uninstall the extension.

7. Security

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but Omninator takes reasonable measures to protect your information — chiefly by not collecting or transmitting it in the first place.

8. Children

The extension is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction) and is not intended for their use.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time, particularly once payments go live. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this policy: open an issue on GitHub.

Early Access

Current

Working today

  • Batch composer — a prompt-per-block textbox (or .txt import) for both Images and Videos.
  • Subject / Scene / Style references — up to 10 across all three, shared by every prompt in the batch.
  • Tag-based chaining — #name a result and pull it back into later prompts with @name to hold a character or product steady.
  • Video frame tags — @start: / @end: for start/end-frame and reference-mode clips.
  • Auto-chaining queue — stack batches with their own settings and let each one kick off the next automatically.
  • Gallery view — results grouped by prompt, with select-all, bulk download, and retry right from the thumbnail.
  • Standalone retry — a failed prompt surfaces its real error and can be relaunched by itself, no touching the rest of the batch.
  • Configurable downloads — a chosen folder plus a naming scheme: numbered, prompt-based, or a custom prefix.
  • Optional watermark removal — strips the corner logo from saved images and videos.
  • First-run tour — a walkthrough of every panel, replayable from Settings whenever you need it again.

On the roadmap

  • A working checkout for the Monthly / Yearly / Lifetime plans shown on the homepage.

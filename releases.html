Early Access

Current

What's in

  • Batch composer — paste or upload a list of prompts, separated by a blank line, for Images or Videos.
  • Reference images — Subject, Scene and Style references, up to 10 total, applied across the whole batch.
  • #name / @name tag chaining — name a generated image and reuse it in later prompts for a consistent character or product.
  • Video frame tags — @start: / @end: to drive start/end-frame and reference-mode clips.
  • Queue with auto-chain — stack multiple batches with different settings; finished batches automatically start the next one.
  • Gallery — every result grouped by prompt, with select-all, bulk download, and per-item retry.
  • Per-prompt retry — a failed prompt shows its real error and can be retried on its own, no rebuilding the batch.
  • Auto-download & naming — results save straight to a configured folder, named numbered, prompt-based, or with a custom prefix.
  • Watermark removal — optional, removes the corner logo from downloaded images and videos.
  • Guided tour — a first-run walkthrough of every panel, replayable anytime from Settings.

Planned

  • Live pricing & checkout for Monthly / Yearly / Lifetime plans.

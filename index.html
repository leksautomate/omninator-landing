How a batch actually runs

Four steps between a list of prompts and a folder full of finished results.

1

Have Flow open

Keep a Google Flow project tab open — Omninator picks up your existing session, nothing extra to configure.

2

Load your prompts

Paste or import your list, and attach reference images if this batch needs them.

3

Launch or stack it

Hit Start to run right away, or send it to the Queue to fire once whatever's running finishes.

4

It files itself

Watch results show up in the Gallery while finished files save straight into your destination folder.

Questions people ask

What exactly does Omninator automate?

It drives Google Flow's own interface for you — typing each prompt, applying your settings, and clicking Generate — so a list turns into a finished batch of images or videos without you sitting there doing it one at a time.

Whose Google Flow account does it use?

Yours. Every generation runs through your own signed-in session and counts against your own quota. Omninator only clicks the buttons you'd click yourself — it never touches Flow's sign-in, safety checks, or generation limits.

Do I have to subscribe?

No — install it free from the Chrome Web Store today. Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime in Pricing above show what paid plans will look like once checkout is live.

What browsers work?

Any Chromium-based browser that supports Manifest V3 extensions, Chrome included.

What about refunds?

Once purchasing is live, refunds will follow the terms on the Refund Policy page.

Terms of use

Omninator automates steps you could already take by hand inside Google Flow, using your own login and your own quota — it doesn't unlock anything you couldn't reach yourself. Prohibited content & acceptable use

Don't use Omninator to generate sexual, pornographic, or otherwise NSFW material. That covers deepfakes or synthetic media depicting a real, identifiable person — public figures included — made without consent, or built to impersonate, deceive, defame, sexualize, or otherwise harm someone. It also covers unsafe or inappropriate depictions of minors, and anything illegal or that violates someone else's rights. Every generation still runs through your own Google Flow account and stays subject to Google's own content and safety rules — Omninator forwards your prompts to Flow and does not disable, weaken, or route around any of Google's moderation. Breaking this rule can get a license suspended or revoked, without a refund.

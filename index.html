How it works

From a list of prompts to a folder of images or videos in four steps.

1

Open Flow

Open a Google Flow project tab — Omninator connects to your own session automatically.

2

Add prompts

Paste or upload your prompts; add reference images if you want.

3

Start or Queue

Pick your settings and hit Start, or add to the Queue to run batches back-to-back.

4

Done

Results land in the Gallery and download automatically, organized by batch.

Frequently asked questions

What does Omninator do?

It's a Chrome extension that automates bulk AI image and video generation in Google Flow. You give it a list of prompts (and optional reference images), and it generates and downloads every image and video for you, organized into folders.

Do I use my own account to generate?

Yes. Generation runs through your own Google Flow session and quota — Omninator only automates Flow's own visible controls (prompt box, generate button, file drops). It doesn't bypass Flow's authentication, safety systems, or generation limits.

Is it a subscription?

You'll be able to choose monthly, yearly, or a one-time lifetime purchase — whichever billing cycle suits you. See Pricing above.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome and other Chromium browsers that support Manifest V3 extensions.
